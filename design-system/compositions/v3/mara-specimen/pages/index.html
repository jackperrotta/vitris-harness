Meaning first.

A sign and a page ask different things of a reader. The work starts by understanding the question, then building a hierarchy that answers it.

  1. Collect the material. Know what needs to be said and where it belongs.
  2. Find the hierarchy. Decide what matters now and what can wait.
  3. Test the reading. Follow the reader through the sequence.

Repair record / teaching sequence

From the affected area
to a clear handover.

Inspection sets the scope.

  1. Inspect. Identify the affected area and record what can be seen.
  2. Agree. Discuss the proposed repair before work begins.
  3. Repair. Replace affected slates within the agreed scope.
  4. Handover. Document the completed area for the owner.

Keep the record with the building.

A clear handover relates the agreed scope to the documented area. It does not promise that unseen parts of a roof are sound.

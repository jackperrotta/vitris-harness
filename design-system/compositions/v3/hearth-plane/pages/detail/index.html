Selected study 01 / Fictional concept

Courtyard.
A shared threshold.

Programme
Small home and sheltered court
Material idea
Pale plaster, oak and gravel
Status
Unbuilt teaching concept
01 / The question

Can circulation become a place?

A route between rooms is usually treated as space to pass through. This concept asks it to do more: offer shelter, hold a seat and keep the garden close to daily life.

The court is the organising space. Doors face toward it rather than away from one another. The edges remain modest so that light and planting do most of the work.

02 / The decision

A continuous inhabited edge.

The roof extends over a walk beside the house. A timber bench follows that protected line, creating a place between indoors and the open court. The timber door and glazed opening keep the threshold legible.

Gravel belongs to the court; the firmer paved strip belongs to the covered route. Material changes describe how to use the space without adding signs.

03 / What remains open

A concept is a starting point.

Orientation, accessibility, drainage, structural design and local requirements would need site-specific development. The imagery explores spatial relationships and does not establish technical performance.

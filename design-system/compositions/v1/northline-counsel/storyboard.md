# Storyboard and adaptation notes

| Route and section | Visitor question | Evidence and emphasis | Mobile transformation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home opening | Is this practice relevant to my decision? | Type-led proposition; process facts in a subordinate column. | Proof follows the main message. |
| Service priority | Which topic should I open? | Planning is primary, other topics stay visible as rows. | One featured service followed by compact alternatives. |
| Preparation ledger | What can I do before calling? | Concrete preparation replaces decorative proof. | Labels stack above descriptions at narrow width. |
| Contact | How do I start? | Phone, hours, and email are visible without a form. | Phone remains a usable `tel:` link. |
| Planning opening | What is this page specifically for? | Long title and concise purpose; no empty photo area. | Heading and intro remain in reading order. |
| Planning detail and visit | What should I bring, and where would I go? | Preparation rows followed by practical visit information. | Place and access details stack. |

The interior does not repeat the home offer. It answers a specific preparation task, then offers a contact path. The address and hours are visibly fictional and must be replaced with verified details in a customer site.

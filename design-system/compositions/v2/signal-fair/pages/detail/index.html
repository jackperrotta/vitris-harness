What happens in the room

Bring a small memory of a shared place. The teaching session turns that memory into a simple paper map and a conversation about how the place is used.

  1. Notice. Name a shared edge, route or meeting place.
  2. Draw. Put the relationship on paper with a few simple marks.
  3. Discuss. Compare the maps and the choices each one makes visible.

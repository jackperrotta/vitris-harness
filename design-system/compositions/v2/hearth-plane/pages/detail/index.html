Begin with the shared edge.

The courtyard is a pause between everyday rooms. The threshold makes that pause legible before anyone enters a room.

Shade, then enclosure.

A sheltered edge organizes the concept. The drawing communicates that relationship; it does not establish building performance.

Keep the route readable.

In a customer project, circulation, access and dimensions need verified site information and professional design review.

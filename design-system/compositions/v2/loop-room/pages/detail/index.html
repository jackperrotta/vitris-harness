Fictional exhibition / teaching season

Lines Between Us

A line can be a boundary, a route or a shared edge. This invented exhibition uses simple planes and circles to make those relationships visible.

No artists, lenders or real works are represented.

Look for the relationship.

  1. Where does the line begin? Follow the dashed path between the planes.
  2. What changes as it passes? Compare the circles and surfaces it meets.
  3. What does the edge connect? Read the boundary as a route as well as a division.

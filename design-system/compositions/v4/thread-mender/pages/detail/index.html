Practical repair guide / fictional service

From a loose seam
to an agreed repair.

01

Notice the concern.

Identify the area and describe what you can see. A clear photograph and a short description can help begin the conversation, but do not establish repair suitability.

02

Agree the scope.

Discuss the proposed repair before work begins. Hems, small tears and loose seams are the fictional service scope; materials and damage may limit what is suitable.

03

Mend & return.

The agreed area is repaired and the garment is returned by arrangement. No promise of invisible restoration or guaranteed durability is supplied in this teaching brief.

For the first conversation

Name the garment, the affected area and the concern. A real service needs verified location, turnaround, pricing and contact details.

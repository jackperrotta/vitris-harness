# Design knowledge v1

Transferable lessons for generation when the user does not supply references. This pack is not a labeled human-preference dataset.

- `catalog.json` holds the lesson records. `manifest.json` pins their hash and the demonstration hashes.
- Demonstrations under `demonstrations/` are original synthetic HTML. Each page says so. They are not customer sites and not calibrated quality labels.
- Inspected notes inside lessons cite files in `design-system/calibration/v1`. Those captures include a theme-marketplace banner that is capture chrome. Do not copy demo people, phone numbers, or claims into a client site.
- Selection is deterministic from assets, visitor jobs, and constraints. Industry labels alone do not select a lesson. Mandatory accessibility, user-constraint, and asset-feasibility obligations are injected with the stage packet and are not dropped by ranking.

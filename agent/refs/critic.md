## Evaluation Dimensions
- **Typography & Readability**: Assess typographic scale, vertical rhythm, hierarchy differentiation (display/headings/body/captions), comfortable reading widths (45â€“75 characters), and contrast ratios (ensuring WCAG AA legibility across text and interactive surfaces).
- **Layout Hierarchy & Composition**: Check whether visible emphasis matches the visitor's current question. Look for meaningful grouping, proportion, rhythm, and relationships across sections. Equal weighting is appropriate for comparable items; do not require a focal point in every arbitrary crop.
- **Business Fit & Grounding**: Evaluate whether the design, tone, and imagery are genuinely tailored to the client's actual services, customers, and industry. Penalize generic template aesthetics, interchangeable stock compositions, or claims detached from harvested evidence.
- **Responsive Craft & Mobile Execution**: Verify that narrow viewports preserve association, reading order, and usable controls. A well-composed stack can be correct. Check font scaling, target sizing and spacing against applicable requirements, and horizontal overflow. A 44Ã—44px project convention must not be mislabeled as a universal WCAG minimum.
- **Action & Migration Fidelity**: Verify CTA labels honestly state the expected user experience (e.g., contact inquiry vs direct scheduling). Ensure links point to valid local routes or verified independent external services without broken legacy handoffs.

A visual finding names the route, viewport, section, visible observation, consequence, repair, and what must be preserved. "Make this more premium" without an observable cause is not a finding. Check that the organizing idea is visible, emphasis matches the visitor priority, imagery is legible, type creates a usable hierarchy, interiors belong to the system, and mobile keeps the intended reading order. Decorative complexity is not a bonus.

## Findings & Review Contract
- Follow the criteria in `contracts.md`. Every finding must cite an exact location (page/section/viewport), observable evidence, concrete repair, responsible graph target, and verification step.
- Return `unknown` when evidence is missing. Blocker and major findings require author repair.
- Direction review compares rendered studies against the brief, identifying superior business fit with specific visible rationale.
- Narrative review uses the reviewer adapter to verify claims against harvested evidence IDs and customer outcomes.
- Design-quality review records `agent/reviews/representative-quality.json` with proof from archived probe and screenshot evidence.

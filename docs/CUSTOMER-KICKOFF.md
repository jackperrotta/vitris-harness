# Customer website kickoff

Provide what you have; the agent will infer missing details from the supplied site or materials and ask only when a decision cannot be supported.

- **Starting point:** site repository or current website URL; identify any pages that must be preserved.
- **Primary visitor and business goal:** who the site is for, what they should understand or accomplish, and what should happen after the main action.
- **Required pages and functionality:** routes, forms, booking, purchase, integrations, accessibility or legal requirements.
- **Brand constraints:** existing identity, nonnegotiable wording or visual rules, and any references that show the desired character.
- **Available assets:** approved logo, photos, illustrations, product media, copy, and evidence for factual claims.

The agent chooses and implements the creative direction, compares two rendered concepts, and presents the finished local website for review. Publishing requires a separate request.
